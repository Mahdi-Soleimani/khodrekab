# CARNO EXPERIENCE STORY MAP

## PHASE 0 — CINEMATIC ENTRY (Pre-scroll)
- **Duration:** 2-4 seconds (On page load)
- **Vehicle Angle:** Hero Frame (Frame 001)
- **Camera/Frame Behavior:** Fixed at frame 001. A subtle cinematic fade-in of the vehicle from a dark/moody state, perhaps using a CSS blur/opacity transition to simulate depth of field focusing.
- **User Psychological State:** Curiosity, awe. "This is different from normal car websites."
- **Main Message:** Carno logomark / Minimalist tagline revealing the brand.
- **UI Elements:** Subtle scroll indicator (e.g., a glowing mouse/arrow icon).
- **CTA Strategy:** No explicit CTA yet. The goal is to draw them into the scroll.

---

## PHASE 1 — DISCOVERY
- **Scroll Percentage:** 0% - 25%
- **Vehicle Angle:** Front Three-Quarter → Front Profile (Frames 001 - 030)
- **Camera/Frame Behavior:** Smooth rotation. The car slowly turns its face toward the user.
- **User Psychological State:** Attraction and curiosity. "What exactly is this platform?"
- **Main Message:** "We help you discover the cars worth buying before everyone else." (قبل از خرید خودرو، چیزهایی وجود دارند که دیده نمی‌شوند.)
- **UI Elements:** Elegant, high-contrast typography appearing as the car rotates. Minimal text.
- **CTA Strategy:** Soft CTA: "شروع کشف" (Start Discovery) pointing downwards to encourage further scrolling.

---

## PHASE 2 — INSPECTION
- **Scroll Percentage:** 25% - 50%
- **Vehicle Angle:** Front Profile → Side Profile (Frames 030 - 060)
- **Camera/Frame Behavior:** Rotation continues revealing the full length of the vehicle.
- **User Psychological State:** Rational evaluation. "Can this actually help me? What is the edge?"
- **Main Message:** "The appearance is not the complete story." (ظاهر، داستان کامل نیست.)
- **UI Elements:** Infographic-style markers or floating data points (Glassmorphism UI) hinting at market analysis, hidden value detection, and opportunity discovery.
- **CTA Strategy:** Passive. Keep the user focused on the story.

---

## PHASE 3 — HIDDEN RISK REVEAL
- **Scroll Percentage:** 50% - 75%
- **Vehicle Angle:** Side Profile → Rear / Rear Three-Quarter (Frames 060 - 090)
- **Camera/Frame Behavior:** Revealing the back of the car, historically the blind spot of a vehicle.
- **User Psychological State:** Shift from generic browsing to a desire for a superior decision. "I want to know what I might be missing."
- **Main Message:** "What others don't see can be the main difference." (چیزی که دیگران نمی‌بینند، می‌تواند تفاوت اصلی باشد.)
- **UI Elements:** Subtle shift in lighting/background color tone (e.g., a slightly darker or intense gradient) to signify depth. Text blocks addressing the desire to find real value and make an informed, confident purchase.
- **CTA Strategy:** Preparation for the final solution. No hard CTA, just building the narrative tension towards a smart decision.

---

## PHASE 4 — SOLUTION + CTA
- **Scroll Percentage:** 75% - 100%
- **Vehicle Angle:** Rear Three-Quarter → Return toward Hero Position (Frames 090 - 120)
- **Camera/Frame Behavior:** The rotation completes its 360-degree journey, bringing the car back to a familiar, empowering angle.
- **User Psychological State:** Resolution, trust, and readiness to act. "Carno helps me make a better decision."
- **Main Message:** "Before you buy, know what others don't see." (قبل از خرید، چیزی را بدانید که دیگران نمی‌بینند.)
- **UI Elements:** Brightened, resolved UI. The data points disappear, leaving a clean view of the car with highly visible, premium buttons.
- **CTA Strategy:** 
  - **Primary:** "رزرو جایگاه" (Reserve Position)
  - **Secondary:** "دریافت اطلاع‌رسانی لانچ" (Get Launch Notifications)
